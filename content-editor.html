HOW TO COLLABORATE

This editor runs in your browser. Your changes are saved locally until you export them.

TO SUBMIT YOUR EDITS:

  1. Make your edits using the editor
  2. Click EXPORT to download your changes as a single file
  3. Email the file to seeyouinfreeiran@gmail.com

SAVING LOCALLY (OPTIONAL):

If you have the project files on your computer, use LOAD to open the data folder, then SAVE to write changes directly.

TIPS:

  1. Use Ctrl/Cmd+S as a shortcut to save
  2. The UNSAVED indicator shows when you have pending changes
  3. Include a note in your email describing what you changed
